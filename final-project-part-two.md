# User research 

## Target audience
> Include your approach to identifying representative individuals, and who you hope to reach with your story. 

Initially, I targeted my research toward stakeholders at armored vehicle manufacturers, such as General Dynamics Land Systems (GDLS). My goal was to inform these individuals about drones as an emerging threat against their tanks. However, I figured this approach would not be the most worthwhile because producers like GDLS already know about drones; their M1E3 Abrams prototype has drone countermeasures. Given this information, along with some of the feedback I was getting from my classmates, I slightly pivoted to target policymakers and analysts who are interested and/or involved in U.S. defense policy.
